# The RPG Blend Briefings

This section contains executive-style engineering briefings used for LinkedIn carousels, leadership discussions, modernization conversations, and operational engineering commentary.

The goal of the briefing series is to provide concise, visually structured insights around:

- Enterprise modernization
- AI adoption
- Architecture governance
- Engineering leadership
- Operational discipline
- Long-lived system stewardship

These briefings are intentionally shorter and more visual than full RPG Blend articles while still maintaining the same operational and editorial tone.
