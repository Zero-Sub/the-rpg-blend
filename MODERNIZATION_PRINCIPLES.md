# The RPG Blend Modernization Principles

## 1. Architecture Must Reduce Operational Risk

Modernization is not cosmetic.

The goal is operational stability, maintainability, delivery speed, and long-term adaptability.

---

## 2. SQL Is the Platform Interface

Modern IBM i architecture should treat SQL as the primary access layer.

Applications should avoid direct physical file access wherever possible.

---

## 3. Business Logic Belongs in Service Boundaries

Business rules should not be duplicated across interactive programs, batch jobs, APIs, and reports.

Encapsulate logic.

---

## 4. Views Create Stability

Views provide abstraction, compatibility, and safer modernization.

Physical tables should not become application contracts.

---

## 5. Operational Discipline Matters More Than Cleverness

Maintainable systems outperform clever systems.

Readable architecture wins.

---

## 6. AI Should Augment Engineering Teams

AI should improve:

- speed
- consistency
- documentation
- onboarding
- analysis
- review quality

AI should not replace engineering judgment.

---

## 7. Delivery Is Part of Architecture

Release strategy, deployment discipline, observability, rollback planning, and operational support are architecture concerns.

---

## 8. Modernization Is Organizational

The largest modernization barriers are usually:

- fear
- process debt
- unclear ownership
- lack of standards
- inconsistent leadership

Technology alone is not enough.
