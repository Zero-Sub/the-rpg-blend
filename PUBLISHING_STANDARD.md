# The RPG Blend Publishing Standard

## Purpose

Every article should strengthen The RPG Blend as a credible IBM i modernization and engineering leadership publication.

The standard is simple: publish work that is practical, specific, and useful to people modernizing long-lived enterprise systems.

---

## Quality Bar

A publishable article must include:

- A clear problem statement
- IBM i, RPG, SQL, architecture, AI adoption, or engineering leadership relevance
- Practical enterprise context
- A point of view
- Operational realism
- Actionable insight

---

## Avoid

Do not publish:

- Generic AI-generated filler
- Empty motivational content
- Buzzword-heavy writing
- Trend-chasing content
- Abstract architecture without implementation reality
- Vendor marketing disguised as engineering guidance

---

## Article Structure

Preferred structure:

1. Today’s Coffee
2. First Sip
3. Problem Definition
4. Operational Reality
5. Modernization Path
6. Implementation Considerations
7. Final Sip

---

## Tone

- Calm
- Experienced
- Strategic
- Direct
- Enterprise-focused
- Practical

No hype.
No fluff.
No fake certainty.
