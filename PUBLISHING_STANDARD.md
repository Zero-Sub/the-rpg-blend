# The RPG Blend Publishing Standard

## Purpose

Every published asset should strengthen The RPG Blend™ as a credible IBM i engineering, modernization, publishing, and education brand.

Publish work that is practical, specific, technically responsible, and useful to people maintaining and modernizing long-lived enterprise systems.

## Quality Bar

A publishable article must include:

- A clear problem, question, or argument
- Relevance to IBM i, RPG, Db2 for i, architecture, AI-assisted development, developer education, production support, or engineering leadership
- Practical enterprise context
- A defensible point of view
- Operational realism
- Actionable insight or a clear decision framework
- Appropriate technical validation and source attribution
- A conclusion that advances the argument rather than merely repeating it

## Evidence and Accuracy

- Distinguish direct experience, documented fact, opinion, and inference.
- Verify release-sensitive commands, product behavior, and technical claims against current primary sources.
- Test code and commands in a safe non-production environment before representing them as validated.
- Do not invent metrics, quotations, case studies, customer examples, or implementation results.
- Remove or generalize employer, client, employee, candidate, system, and production details unless public use is explicitly approved.

## Editorial Structure

Structure should follow the argument rather than a mandatory formula.

Common modules include:

1. Strong opening or **First Sip**
2. Problem definition
3. Operational or business reality
4. Technical explanation
5. Practical path forward
6. Implementation and adoption considerations
7. Risks, limitations, or trade-offs
8. Final takeaway or **Final Sip**

Not every article needs every module. Headings should describe the actual content rather than forcing generic labels onto it.

## Coffee Motif

Coffee is an editorial motif, not the subject of the brand.

- A coffee reference is optional.
- When used, place it in the opening or closing and connect it directly to the article’s argument.
- Do not require both **Today’s Coffee** and **First Sip**.
- Avoid extended tasting notes unless the publication context genuinely benefits from them.
- Do not let coffee language delay the technical point or make the brand look like a coffee company.
- Academy material may use coffee more visibly when it supports continuity, friendliness, or the sample application.

## Voice

- Experienced
- Calm
- Strategic
- Direct
- Technically credible
- Educational without being academic
- Enterprise-focused
- Practical rather than promotional

No hype.  
No filler.  
No fake certainty.  
No vendor marketing disguised as engineering guidance.

## Avoid

Do not publish:

- Generic AI-generated filler
- Empty motivational content
- Trend-chasing content
- Abstract architecture without implementation reality
- Reckless rewrite advocacy
- Tool claims that ignore testing, ownership, security, or governance
- Unreviewed production details or proprietary material
- Claims that The RPG Blend Academy or a publication is complete before its validation gate is met

## Canonical Content and Distribution

- The approved Markdown asset in this repository is the canonical public source.
- The website, Substack, and LinkedIn versions may be adapted for channel length and presentation.
- Adaptations must preserve the central argument, facts, legal language, and authorial position.
- Final public URLs and publication dates should be recorded in the editorial workflow.
- Drafts, research, transcripts, and manuscript working files may remain in Google Drive or The RPG Blend Studio until approved.

## AI-Assisted Writing

AI may support research organization, outlining, editing, technical review, consistency checks, and alternative phrasing.

The author remains responsible for:

- Accuracy
- Originality
- Technical judgment
- Voice
- Source verification
- Privacy
- Copyright compliance
- Final approval

Do not publish AI output that has not been reviewed line by line for substance and accuracy.

## Publication Review Gate

Before publication, confirm:

- [ ] The argument is clear and specific.
- [ ] The opening reaches the point quickly.
- [ ] Real examples are safe to publish and appropriately generalized.
- [ ] Technical claims are validated or explicitly qualified.
- [ ] IBM and third-party trademarks are used appropriately.
- [ ] Brand names and imprint language match `BRAND_GUIDE.md`.
- [ ] The article is stored in the correct canonical category and filename.
- [ ] Links and relative paths work.
- [ ] The channel adaptation preserves the canonical meaning.
- [ ] The call to action is relevant and restrained.
- [ ] The final copy has received human approval.
