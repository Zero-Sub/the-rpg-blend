# The RPG Blend Brand Guide

## Positioning

The RPG Blend is an enterprise IBM i modernization and engineering leadership publication focused on practical modernization, RPG and SQL architecture, AI adoption, operational discipline, and leadership inside long-lived enterprise systems.

## Brand Characteristics

The publication should feel:

- Experienced
- Calm
- Strategic
- Technical
- Editorial
- Enterprise-ready
- Trustworthy
- Modern without feeling trendy

The publication should not feel:

- Playful coffee shop branding
- Retro gaming
- Cyberpunk
- Startup neon
- Cartoonish
- Meme culture
- Generic AI hype

## Content Pillars

- IBM i modernization
- RPG architecture
- SQL engineering
- Service programs
- Operational modernization
- AI adoption inside enterprise development
- Engineering leadership
- Team effectiveness
- Delivery discipline
- Technical debt reduction

## Voice

- First-person experience driven
- Opinionated but practical
- Executive-level clarity
- No fluff
- No hype
- Short paragraphs
- Strong structure
- Real operational examples

## Visual Identity

Primary colors:

- Deep Green: #2F7A32
- RPG Orange: #F28C28
- Soft Cream: #F5E7C6
- Black Coffee: #1E1E1E

Visual themes:

- Editorial
- Architectural
- Warm enterprise aesthetic
- Coffee and engineering blend
- IBM i modernization imagery
