## Distribution Package for Each Major Article

A major article should normally produce:

1. Canonical Markdown article in this repository
2. Website version or content-collection entry
3. Substack publication version
4. LinkedIn article or teaser version
5. One supporting visual, diagram, or social card when useful
6. Final public URLs recorded in the workflow
7. Follow-up ideas captured rather than embedded into the same article

## Editorial Cadence

Use a sustainable cadence rather than publishing to satisfy an arbitrary weekly quota.

Recommended pattern:

- One substantial article or book/Academy milestone at a time
- Supporting LinkedIn or Substack content derived from completed canonical work
- No new major draft while the current item lacks a clear next action
- Weekend work may be lighter and focused on review, reading, family, and long-term creative progress

## Selection Rules

Choose the next item based on:

1. Strategic relevance to IBM i engineering, modernization, education, or leadership
2. Connection to the current book, Academy, or website milestone
3. Availability of real examples and technical evidence
4. Readiness for publication rather than novelty alone
5. Ability to complete the full distribution package

## Content Boundaries

Do not publish:

- Private employer, employee, candidate, client, or production details
- Unvalidated technical claims presented as proven
- Book content that undermines the value of the finished publication
- Academy claims that imply uncompleted validation or launch status
- Coffee references that overwhelm the engineering message
