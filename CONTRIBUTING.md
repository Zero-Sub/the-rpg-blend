# Contributing to The RPG Blend

## Purpose

The RPG Blend exists to document practical modernization, operational discipline, engineering leadership, and IBM i architectural thinking.

Contributions should strengthen clarity, operational realism, and technical credibility.

---

## Contribution Standards

Good contributions are:

- Practical
- Specific
- Experience-driven
- Operationally grounded
- Technically accurate
- Maintainable

Avoid:

- Buzzword-heavy writing
- Vendor marketing language
- Generic AI-generated filler
- Hype-driven modernization claims
- Unrealistic architecture proposals

---

## Preferred Topics

- IBM i modernization
- RPG architecture
- SQL engineering
- Stored procedures
- Service programs
- Operational governance
- Release management
- AI adoption
- Engineering leadership
- Delivery systems

---

## Writing Expectations

Preferred characteristics:

- Short paragraphs
- Clear structure
- Strong operational context
- Real enterprise examples
- Leadership perspective where appropriate

Use the templates in `/templates` whenever possible.

---

## Philosophy

The RPG Blend prioritizes:

- clarity over hype
- maintainability over cleverness
- operational discipline over chaos
- modernization with realistic governance
- long-term system stewardship
