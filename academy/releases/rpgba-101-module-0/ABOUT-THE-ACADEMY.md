# About The RPG Blend Academy

## The RPG Blend

**The RPG Blend** is an IBM i engineering, modernization, and education brand focused on helping developers and teams work more effectively on the platform.

Its work brings together practical technical guidance, engineering leadership, modernization strategy, responsible AI-assisted development, and developer education. The guidance is grounded in real enterprise IBM i environments, where reliability, security, maintainability, testing, and production support matter as much as writing code.

## The education division

**The RPG Blend Academy is the education division of The RPG Blend.**

The Academy prepares developers to contribute safely and effectively to modern IBM i teams. Its primary learners include:

- college graduates beginning an Associate RPG Developer path;
- developers transferring from another technology platform;
- existing IBM i developers modernizing their skills and workflow.

## How the Academy teaches

The Academy uses a modern-first development workflow centered on:

- Visual Studio Code;
- Code for IBM i;
- Db2 for i extension tooling;
- Git and GitHub workflows;
- IBM i Access Client Solutions when it remains the appropriate tool;
- IBM Bob for responsible AI-assisted development.

Legacy tools and terminology are explained when learners need to recognize, maintain, or support them, but they are not treated as the default development experience.

## IBM Bob and learner responsibility

IBM Bob is integrated into the learning path as an engineering assistant. Learners use it to explain unfamiliar code, trace logic and dependencies, diagnose errors, suggest tests, review changes, and support modernization.

Bob does not replace understanding. Learners remain responsible for:

- technical accuracy;
- security and privacy;
- testing;
- code quality;
- source control;
- change management;
- validating every accepted AI-assisted result.

## Original course content

IBM, COMMON, vendor, community, and book materials are used as reference sources to validate terminology, technical accuracy, coverage, and learning progression. Academy lessons, explanations, examples, diagrams, code, labs, quizzes, assessments, and capstone work are original.

## About George VanEaton

George VanEaton is an IBM i engineering leader with more than 30 years of experience in RPG, Db2 for i, SQL, software architecture, modernization, and production support.

He created The RPG Blend to share practical IBM i engineering guidance and founded The RPG Blend Academy to help prepare the next generation of IBM i developers. He is also a contributing author to the IBM Redbooks work-in-progress publication **Modernizing IBM i Applications**.
