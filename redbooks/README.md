# IBM Redbooks Contributions

## George VanEaton

George VanEaton is an IBM i engineering leader, author of **The RPG Blend**, creator of **The RPG Blend Academy**, and a contributing author to the IBM Redbooks work-in-progress publication *Modernizing IBM i Applications* (MD260020).

> **Publication status:** Work in progress. This designation must remain in place until IBM formally completes and publishes the work.

## Contribution Focus

George's IBM Redbooks contribution and broader professional work center on:

- IBM i application modernization
- Modern RPG engineering and modular architecture
- Db2 for i and SQL-first development
- AI-assisted code understanding and impact analysis
- IBM Bob and contemporary development workflows
- Engineering standards and modernization governance
- Knowledge transfer and IBM i workforce development
- Enterprise healthcare application development and support

## Professional Position

George's modernization philosophy is grounded in a practical principle:

> Modernization is not a rewrite. It is the disciplined transformation of how teams understand, design, document, test, govern, and evolve IBM i systems.

His work combines more than 30 years of IBM i, RPG, Db2, SQL, architecture, production-support, and engineering-leadership experience with current work in AI adoption, developer development, technical standards, and application modernization.
