# The RPG Blend Roadmap

**Last aligned:** August 5, 2026

## Operating Model

- `Zero-Sub/the-rpg-blend` is the public, canonical repository for approved articles, frameworks, Academy source, publishing standards, and reusable brand material.
- `Zero-Sub/the-rpg-blend-site` is the private website-development repository.
- Google Drive and The RPG Blend Studio are working environments for drafts, research, planning, manuscripts, and production files.
- Substack and LinkedIn are distribution channels, not canonical storage.
